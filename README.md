## 🔍 Key Findings

- **Online** was the leading sales channel at approximately **27.75M**.
- **Accessories** generated approximately **36M** in sales.
- **VIP customers** contributed approximately **29.62M** in sales.
- The reported profit margin reached **32.65%**.
